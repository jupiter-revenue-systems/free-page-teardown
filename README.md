# Page Teardown

Drop in your sales page link. Your AI grades it like a buying psychologist: the promise, the proof, where the price sits, what's missing. You get your top 5 changes.

## The promise

When you finish, you'll know why visitors leave without buying, and which 5 changes to make first.

## Who it's for

- Coaches, creators and agencies with a sales page that gets visits but few buyers.
- Anyone who has read their own page so many times they can't see it anymore.

## What's inside

- `START_HERE.md`: the instructions your AI reads first. It asks a few questions, then tears the page down part by part.
- `templates/grading-sheet.md`: the 6 parts and what earns each grade.
- `templates/teardown-report.md`: the report layout, ending in your top 5 changes.
- `templates/fresh-eyes-check.md`: a short prompt to run in a new chat after you change something.

## How to use it

1. Download this pack and unzip it.
2. Take phone screenshots of your sales page, top to bottom, in order.
3. Open a new chat in Claude or ChatGPT. Add the pack folder and your link or screenshots.
4. Type: `Read START_HERE.md and follow it.`
5. Save the report. Copy your current page words into a doc before you change anything.
6. Make change number 1, then run `templates/fresh-eyes-check.md` in a new chat.

## What it doesn't do

- It reads the page. It can't see your visitor numbers.
- It names the changes. It doesn't rewrite or rebuild the page.
- It can't test your checkout or how fast the page loads.
- A grade is a trained read, not a test with real buyers.
