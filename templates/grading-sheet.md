# Grading sheet

Read the page as a stranger on a phone. Grade each part from 1 to 5. Every grade needs a quote from the page.

- 5: a stranger gets it right away
- 3: it's there, but they have to work for it
- 1: it's missing or it confuses them

## 1. The promise (the first screen only)

What shows before anyone scrolls.

- Can I tell in 5 seconds what I'll get?
- Can I tell it's for someone like me?
- Is it about my outcome, or about the product's name and features?
- Does it match what I saw before I got here (the ad, the DM, the post)?
- Is there a button on this first screen?

## 2. The proof

A reason to believe the promise.

- Is there any real proof: a result, a named person, a screenshot, a number with a source, years of doing it?
- Is the proof close to the claim it backs up, or parked far down the page?
- Is it specific? "Helped lots of people" isn't proof.
- Does the person behind the offer show up as a real human?

## 3. The offer

What I get when I pay.

- Could I list what I get after one read?
- Is each piece described by what it does for me, not just what it's called?
- Do I know how I get it and when?
- Is it clear what I have to do, and how much time it takes me?

## 4. The price

Where it sits and what comes before it.

- Where does the price first show up on the way down?
- By that point, have I seen the promise, some proof and what I get?
- Is the price easy to find, or do I have to hunt for it?
- Is it plain what kind of payment it is: one time, monthly, a plan?
- Is there anything that makes the price feel fair, in honest terms?

## 5. The ask

The button and the path to it.

- Is there one main thing to click, or several competing?
- Do the button words say what happens next?
- Does the button come back after each big section, with the same words?
- Are there links that pull me off the page before I buy?

## 6. What's missing

The doubts the page never answers. Check each one:

- Who is this NOT for?
- What happens right after I pay?
- How long until I see something?
- What if it doesn't fit me? (Refund terms, stated plainly either way.)
- What do I need to have or know before I start?
- Who do I ask if I'm stuck?
- Why this, and why now, without pressure tricks?

List every doubt that has no answer on the page.

## Reading on a phone

Note these as you go. They don't get their own grade, but they often explain a low one.

- Text too small or too long before the first button
- Pictures that push the promise below the first screen
- Sections that repeat the same point
