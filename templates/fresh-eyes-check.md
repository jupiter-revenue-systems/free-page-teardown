# Fresh-eyes check

Run this after you make a change. Use a brand new chat, so the AI hasn't seen your old page or your teardown. Attach a new phone screenshot of the part you changed.

## For the top of the page

```
You're a stranger on a phone who has never heard of this business. Here's the top of a sales page.

In 5 seconds, tell me:
1. What do I get?
2. Who is it for?
3. What am I supposed to do next?

If you can't tell, say which part is unclear. Don't be kind. Be accurate.
```

## For any other section

```
You're a stranger on a phone who has never heard of this business. Here's one section of a sales page.

Tell me:
1. What is this section trying to make me believe?
2. Do I believe it? What on the screen is the reason?
3. What question do I still have after reading it?

Short answers. Don't rewrite anything.
```

## How to read the answer

- If the stranger's answer matches what you meant, the change worked as a read. Leave it and watch your sales.
- If it doesn't match, the page is still saying something else. Fix that one part and run the check again in another new chat.
- If the new version does worse with real buyers, put back the old words from your restore point.
